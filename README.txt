HOW TO PUT IT ONLINE (free, about 10 minutes)
  Option A — Netlify
    1. Go to app.netlify.com/drop and sign in.
    2. Drag this whole folder onto the page. It goes live at once on a free netlify.app address.
    3. Site settings > Domain management > Add a domain > ruvo.krd, then follow the DNS steps it shows.
  Option B — Cloudflare Pages
    1. dash.cloudflare.com > Workers & Pages > Create > Pages > Upload assets.
    2. Upload this folder, then add the custom domain ruvo.krd.

Domain
  Register ruvo.krd through a .krd registrar (the .krd registry lists accredited registrars),
  then point it to Netlify or Cloudflare as above. The site already uses https://ruvo.krd
  in its links, sitemap and share previews.
